# GOTronome drum styles. Shared byte-for-byte with the iOS app.
#
# style  <id> <display name>
# groove <time signature> <steps per beat> <one token per step>
#
# bass   <time signature> <steps per beat> <one token per step, one or more bars>
#
# A groove token is the set of voices struck on that step, or "." for a rest:
#   K kick   S snare   H closed hi-hat   P pedal hi-hat   R ride   X cross-stick
# A bass token is a semitone offset from the chosen root, or "." for a rest.
# "|" may separate bars and is ignored. A bass line follows the groove of the
# same time signature and may span several bars; it cycles with the measure.
# The metronome style has no grooves: it plays the per-beat accent pattern
# and is valid in every time signature. Styles appear in file order.

style metronome Metronome

style rock Rock
groove 4/4 2 KH H SH H KH H SH H
bass   4/4 2 0 0 0 0 7 7 0 0
groove 6/8 1 KH H H SH H H
bass   6/8 1 0 . . 7 . .

style swing Swing
groove 4/4 3 R . . RP . R R . . RP . R
bass   4/4 1 0 4 7 9 | 10 9 7 -1

style shuffle Shuffle
groove 4/4 3 KH . H SH . H KH . H SH . H
bass   4/4 3 0 . 0 0 . 0 7 . 7 0 . 0

style waltz Waltz
groove 3/4 1 KH XH XH
bass   3/4 1 0 7 7

style bossa Bossa
groove 4/4 4 KXH . H X H . KXH . KH . XH . XH . KH .
bass   4/4 4 0 . . . . . 7 . 0 . . . . . 7 .

style march March
groove 2/4 2 K S KS S
bass   2/4 2 0 . 7 .
groove 2/2 2 K S KS S
bass   2/2 2 0 . 7 .
